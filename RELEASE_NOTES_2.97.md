# HYPERION DIGITAL EX 2.97

Store product pages now show each title's catalog developer (for example FIREOUT ONLINE shows Developer EX ROMANIA and Publisher EX Inc.) instead of a hard-coded EX Inc. developer. A per-title license line is only shown from the catalog `license` field, and a HYPERION license is hidden when a title sets its own developer or publisher, so FIREOUT ONLINE does not show one.
